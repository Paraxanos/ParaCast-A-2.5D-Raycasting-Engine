# Map format

Sector maps are plain text, one directive per line. `#` starts a comment; blank lines,
tabs and CRLF line endings are fine. Numbers are decimals (`-0.25`, `3`, `12.5`) turned
into 16.16 fixed point by the loader, with no floating point involved.

```
player 2 4 0                       # x y yaw_degrees   (exactly one)

sector 0 floor 0 ceil 3.2 ftex 4 ctex 5 light 26
  v 0 0 mid 0                      # one line per vertex, in order around the sector
  v 12 0 portal 1 up 2 low 2
  v 12 8 mid 1
  v 0 8 mid 0
end

thing 4 2 0                        # x y sprite
```

## Sectors

`sector N` starts a sector. Ids must be `0, 1, 2, ...` in order. Attributes are key/value
pairs on the same line:

| Key | Meaning | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `floor`, `ceil` | heights in world units (required) | none |
| `ftex`, `ctex` | floor and ceiling texture ids | 4, 5 |
| `light` | 0 (dark) to 31 (full bright) | 24 |

The ceiling must be at least 0.5 above the floor. Floors and ceilings are flat.

`v x y [portal N] [mid T] [up T] [low T]` adds a vertex. Its attributes describe the
**edge that starts at that vertex**, running to the next vertex (the last vertex's edge
runs back to the first). Closing the polygon is implicit; `end` finishes the sector.

| Attribute | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `portal N` | the edge is open onto sector N instead of being a solid wall |
| `mid T` | texture of a solid wall (default 0) |
| `up T`, `low T` | textures of the upper and lower wall pieces beside a portal (default: `mid`) |

Upper and lower pieces appear wherever the neighbour's ceiling is lower or its floor is
higher than this sector's, as in Doom.

## Rules the loader enforces

- **Convex sectors only.** Concave rooms are built from several convex sectors joined
  by portals (see `nested.map`). Collinear vertices are allowed and are how a wall is
  split so part of it can be a portal.
- **Winding does not matter.** Clockwise sectors are reversed on load, carrying each
  edge's attributes with it, so the interior is always on the left of every edge.
- **Portals must mirror.** If sector A has an edge from P to Q with `portal B`, sector B
  must have an edge from Q to P with `portal A`, with exactly those endpoints. A long
  wall shared with two smaller sectors is split at the same vertices on both sides.
- Edges are at least 1/16 unit long; coordinates are within ±512 and heights within ±256
  (so every cross product and projection fits its integer type).
- Textures ids are `0 brick, 1 tile, 2 stone, 3 wood, 4 floor, 5 ceiling, 6 metal,
  7 carpet, 8 sign`. The sign is an arrow pointing toward increasing wall position: if it
  reads backwards on screen, wall texturing is mirrored.
- Sprite ids are `0 barrel, 1 lamp, 2 pillar`.
- At most 256 sectors, 4096 walls, 64 vertices per sector and 256 things.

Errors name the line: `maze.map: line 14: sector is not convex`. Problems found only
after reading the whole file (a portal with no mirror, a thing outside every sector)
have no line number.

## Things and the player

`thing x y sprite` places a billboard standing on the floor of whichever sector contains
it. Things are decoration in Phase 2: they do not move, collide or block.

`player x y yaw` is the spawn point; yaw is in degrees, 0 facing +x, increasing toward +y.
The eye is 0.8 above the floor.

## Movement

The player is a circle of radius 0.25. A wall blocks it, and so does a portal whose
neighbour's floor is more than 0.5 higher than the current floor (a step too tall to
climb) or whose opening is lower than 1.2 (too low to stand in). Drops of any depth are
allowed.

## Shipped maps

| Map | What it exercises |
| --- | --- |
| `steps` | stairs (lower walls), lowered ceilings (upper walls), a pit below the hall, the sign texture |
| `window` | a doorway and a window in a one-unit wall: chains of portals, a sill and lintel, an unwalkable gap |
| `nested` | a raised, low-ceilinged dais inside a ring of five sectors; several portals onto one neighbour |
| `maze` | 36 rooms with 2-unit doorways, varied heights and light; generated by `tools/gen_maze.py` |
| `corridor` | 24 sectors in a row, every shared edge open: the view runs through 24 portals. Benchmark scene; generated by `tools/gen_stress.py` |
| `hall` | one 80x80 sector with 220 sprites: almost no traversal, heavy floor/ceiling spans and sprites. Benchmark scene; generated by `tools/gen_stress.py` |
